Name matching ignores uppercase and lowercase. Enter your name as it appears on your ID.

The photo does not need to be HD, but the name and face must be readable. The text check compares the name you entered and recognizable document labels with the selected ID type. It does not authenticate the document or verify its security features or expiry. Text scanning runs locally on this server; the ID image and selfie are sent to the configured face-matching service for comparison.

Live face check

Allow camera access, then follow the prompts to turn your head left and right and blink. Keep your face in view throughout the check.

Complete the ID image check before starting the camera.

After you submit, the server will compare the captured face with the face on the uploaded ID. Registration is rejected if the match is below 70% similarity or the verification service cannot confirm it.
